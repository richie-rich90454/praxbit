# Ethical engagement

Praxbit should be enjoyable because the learning is satisfying, not because leaving is made uncomfortable. Engagement is measured by useful sessions and durable understanding, not by time in app.

## Allowed

- Short, complete interactions.
- Visible conceptual progress.
- Meaningful achievements tied to evidence.
- A continuing story and recognizable characters.
- Optional practice and review.
- Personalization by interest and challenge level.
- Cooperative club events and optional competition.
- Immediate, explanatory feedback.
- Creative projects and exploration.
- Clear goals and a stopping point.
- Gentle reminders only when the learner enables them and outside quiet hours.
- Optional streaks that never punish a missed day.

## Not allowed

- Punitive streak loss, streak freezes sold as relief, or “don’t break your streak” pressure.
- Shame-based copy, red failure badges, or public failure.
- Artificial scarcity, countdowns, “limited seats”, or fake urgency.
- Energy, lives, or timers that block learning.
- Rewards for session duration, repeated opens, or idle time.
- Loot boxes, randomized paid rewards, or pay-to-win.
- Aggressive push notifications, re-engagement spam, or guilt.
- Dark patterns: hidden unsubscribe, confusing consent, pre-checked AI, or a difficult account deletion.
- Leaderboards based on unverified local submissions.
- Public profiles, unrestricted chat, or direct messaging without moderation.
- Claims of mastery, certification, or competition verification that are not true.
- AI used as the sole judge of correctness or as a replacement for understanding.

## Notifications and reminders

- Off by default.
- Opt-in with a clear description of what will be sent and when.
- Quiet hours default to 22:00–08:00.
- No notification for a missed day.
- No notification that implies the learner is falling behind.
- A single daily reminder is the maximum unless the learner explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Every notification has an immediate off switch.

## Streaks

- A streak counts days with a meaningful practice or completion.
- A missed day resets the current streak and preserves the longest streak.
- No XP, mastery, content, or standing is lost.
- No “streak repair” purchase.
- The streak is not shown on the Learn screen as the primary metric.

## Competition

- Club events are structured and verified server-side.
- Leaderboards show verified points, not raw client claims.
- A local companion result is explicitly untrusted.
- Losing does not remove learning progress.
- There is no paid advantage.
- A learner can opt out of the leaderboard without losing club membership.

## AI

- Off by default.
- Explicit consent before provider requests.
- Bounded context and a privacy notice.
- The learner can turn it off at any time.
- AI output is labeled as generated and is not the authority.
- The product teaches review and verification, not blind acceptance.
- Do not use AI to generate manipulative copy or fake encouragement.

## Children and students

- Guest mode is the default for learners below the applicable digital-consent age.
- Do not collect real names, school names, precise locations, or contact details without the required safeguards.
- Do not add public social features without moderation and reporting.
- Do not use persuasive design aimed at children to increase time or spending.

## Metrics

Measure:

- Completion of meaningful learning objectives.
- Independent transfer success.
- Review retention.
- Time to first useful action.
- Error recovery.
- Accessibility task success.
- Learner-reported usefulness.
- Support and moderation load.

Do not optimize solely for daily active users, session length, or streak length. A learner who completes a useful five-minute session and leaves is a success.

## Review questions

Before shipping an engagement feature, ask:

1. Does it help the learner understand something?
2. Would it still be acceptable if the learner used it for five minutes and left?
3. Does it work without spending money?
4. Can it be turned off?
5. Does it respect sleep, school, work, and caregiving?
6. Would we be comfortable explaining the mechanic to a parent or teacher?
7. Does it create a false claim of mastery, verification, or urgency?

If the answer is no, do not ship it.
